# Delete your Kinroute account

## Delete from the app

1. Open Kinroute and go to **Account**.
2. Tap **Delete my account**.
3. Read the confirmation, then confirm deletion.

Kinroute stops location sharing and removes your account profile, location records, and circles that you own. Members lose access to a circle you own. Your purchases table may retain the product, subscription state, and expiry needed for accounting, while personal fields and linked purchase tokens are cleared. Backups expire under the hosting provider's normal backup cycle.

You do not need to email us to delete an account you can access in the app. If you cannot access the app or need help, contact **klixupwork@gmail.com** with the account email if you linked one, or your Kinroute username. Do not send a password, verification code, or location history.

## Delete location sharing without deleting your account

You can pause sharing or leave a circle from Kinroute. Pausing stops new location collection for that circle. Leaving removes your membership and circle access. Existing history follows the retention period described in the [privacy policy](./).
